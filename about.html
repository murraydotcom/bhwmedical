About us

Whole-person care, rooted in Baltimore

Baltimore Healthcare and Wellness — BHW Medical Group — was built on a simple idea: your health is connected, so your care should be too.

We bring primary care, behavioral health, vascular care, pediatric support, and chronic-care management together under one team — so you're treated as a whole person, not a stack of separate problems.

That means your primary provider, your mental-health support, and your chronic-care team actually talk to each other. It means care continues between visits, through check-ins, monitoring, and a direct line to the office. And it means education is part of the plan — because understanding your health is what makes managing it possible.

We're proud to serve Baltimore City and the surrounding counties, and to welcome new patients across most insurance plans.
