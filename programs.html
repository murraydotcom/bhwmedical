Our programs

One team, coordinated across every kind of care

Whatever brings you in, your care connects. Here's how each BHW program works — and how they work together.

Primary Care

Your medical home

Comprehensive care for adults and families — the steady relationship that keeps everything else on track.

  • Annual wellness visits and preventive screenings
  • Sick visits and same-week concerns
  • Management of ongoing conditions like diabetes, blood pressure, and thyroid
  • Referrals and coordination with specialists — handled by us
Mind & Mood

Behavioral health, woven in

Mental health is health. Our integrated behavioral and recovery support lives alongside your primary care — so you're never bounced between disconnected offices.

  • Depression, anxiety, and stress support
  • Substance-use and recovery care with a compassionate team
  • Coordinated medication management with your primary provider
  • Warm hand-offs, not cold referrals
Flow

Vascular & circulation care

Healthy circulation keeps you moving comfortably. Flow provides evaluation, monitoring, and vein-health support close to home.

  • Evaluation of leg pain, swelling, and vein concerns
  • Vascular screening and ongoing monitoring
  • Care coordinated with your primary and chronic-care team
CharmEd Minds

Support for kids & families

CharmEd helps children and teens build focus, resilience, and healthy routines — with families as partners in the plan.

  • Executive-function and attention support
  • Behavioral and developmental guidance for parents
  • School- and home-friendly strategies
Chronic Care Management

Care that continues between visits

For ongoing conditions, our CCM, APCM, and remote-monitoring programs give you a care team that checks in regularly — catching small problems before they become big ones.

  • Regular check-ins from a dedicated care team
  • Remote monitoring for blood pressure and other vitals where appropriate
  • Help with medications, refills, and coordinating appointments
  • A direct line to the office through your patient portal
